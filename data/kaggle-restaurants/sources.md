# Sources

The GRFDI dataset is synthetically generated to mimic real-world distributions and ensure absolute legal compliance without violating Terms of Service of major food delivery platforms.

However, the statistical distributions and bounds used to generate this data are heavily informed by open data available from:
1. **OpenStreetMap (OSM)**: For realistic geographic bounding boxes of major cities.
2. **OpenFoodFacts**: For understanding the macro-nutrient bounds of standard food categories.
3. **World Bank Open Data**: For country income groups and GDP per capita estimates informing price distributions.
